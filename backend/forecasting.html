Forecasting

The course covers the details starting from basics of forecasting to development of complex forecasting models in Pharmaceutical industry. The course would start with developing your basics related to Pharmaceutical value chain and would deep dive into Forecasting concepts. The course would also help you learn technical tools required to develop commercial forecast models. At the end of the course you would have detailed knowledge and experience into projects related to development of complex forecasting models for Pharma brands and would be ready to work in the Forecasting profile in large Pharmaceutical firms.

What will the course cover

Industry Overview for Pharmaceuticals
Basics of Forecasting
Knowledge on Forecasting and Statistical Models
Technical Tools Overview including Advanced Excel, VBA
Applications of Forecasting to Pharmaceutical Use Cases
Visualization of Forecasts using Tools like PowerBI, Tableau, Qlik
